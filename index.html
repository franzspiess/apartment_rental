The Neighbourhood

Proper Vienna — and the city centre is closer than you think

The 20th district is one of Vienna's most genuinely Viennese neighbourhoods — not a tourist zone, not a gentrified shell. You're on Wallensteinplatz, directly at the edge of the Augarten, with excellent connections to the whole city and the kind of street-level life that makes Vienna what it is.

City Centre

🌳 The Augarten

One of Vienna's great baroque parks, laid out in the 17th century and opened to the public in 1775 by Emperor Joseph II. Spanning 52 hectares, it's home to the Vienna Porcelain Manufactory (Augarten Porzellan, founded 1718 — one of the oldest in Europe), the Vienna Boys' Choir residence in the Augartenpalais, ancient chestnut meadows, and two towering WWII Flak towers that loom as a quiet counterpoint to all that imperial grace. It's your front garden.

☕ Food & Coffee

Café Vindobona is the neighbourhood anchor — a proper Viennese coffee house for breakfast, reading, or a long afternoon. The area has some of Vienna's best Turkish restaurants alongside solid Austrian, Mexican, and Italian options. The Hannovermarkt is a short walk away for fresh produce and daily shopping. Plenty of supermarkets and shops in every direction.

🚇 Getting Around

You're well connected. The U4 and U6 are a short walk, with quick interchange to U2 and U1 — putting you at Stephansplatz or the Ringstrasse in under 15 minutes. Praterstern is nearby: from there you can reach the main train stations and Vienna Airport directly. The city centre is also a pleasant 30-minute walk along the Donaukanal.

🍺 Bars & Evenings

A relaxed local bar scene rather than a nightlife strip. Tabula Rasa and Café Frame are neighbourhood favourites. Good spots to nurse a wine or a beer without the tourist markup of the 1st district.
