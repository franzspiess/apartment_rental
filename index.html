The Neighbourhood

Proper Vienna — and the city centre is closer than you think

The 20th district is one of Vienna's most genuinely Viennese neighbourhoods — not a tourist zone, not a gentrified shell. You're on Wallensteinplatz, directly at the edge of the Augarten, with excellent connections to the whole city and the kind of street-level life that makes Vienna what it is.

City Centre

🌳 The Augarten

One of Vienna's great baroque parks — 52 hectares, opened to the public in 1775 by Emperor Joseph II. Home to the Vienna Porcelain Manufactory (founded 1718), the Vienna Boys' Choir residence, ancient chestnut meadows, and two towering WWII Flak towers. Your front garden.

🍽 Food & Shops

Some of Vienna's best Turkish restaurants alongside solid Austrian, Mexican, and Italian options. The Hannovermarkt is a short walk for fresh produce. Plenty of supermarkets and everyday shops in every direction.

🚇 Getting Around

The U4 and U6 are a short walk, with a quick ride connecting you to U2 and U1 — putting you at Stephansplatz or the Ringstrasse in minutes. Praterstern is nearby for direct trains to the main stations and Vienna Airport. The city centre is also a pleasant 30-minute walk along the Donaukanal.

🍺 Bars & Evenings

A relaxed local bar scene — Tabula Rasa and Café Frame are neighbourhood favourites. Good spots for a wine or a beer without the tourist markup of the 1st district.
